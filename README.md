# Team Quiz

A free, Kahoot-style quiz for your team on Zoom. You host on your laptop and share the screen. Players join from their own browser with a 6-digit Game PIN, a nickname and a cartoon character.

There's nothing to install and no server to run. Your browser runs the game. Every message between you and the players goes through the free PeerJS server over a normal secure web connection (port 443), the same way websites talk. Company networks and VPNs block direct laptop-to-laptop links but allow this (see `js/link.js`).

## Putting it online / updating it

The site lives on Netlify. After any change, upload the **whole `TeamQuiz` folder** again: open your site in Netlify, go to **Deploys**, and drag the folder onto the drop area. Your saved quizzes are kept, because they live in your browser, not on Netlify.

- Host page: `https://YOUR-SITE.netlify.app/host.html`
- Player page: `https://YOUR-SITE.netlify.app/`

## Making a quiz

- **With Claude:** click **🤖 Prompt for Claude**, paste it into Claude, and replace `[TOPIC]` and `[NUMBER]`. Then click **📋 Paste from Claude** and paste Claude's whole reply. Claude also picks a matching background theme. The prompt text is also in `CLAUDE-PROMPT.md`.
- **Background theme:** in **✏️ Edit**, choose one of 10 photo backgrounds, choose **Change every question**, or upload your own picture. Players' screens show the same background. If you use your own picture, players see the Galaxy theme instead.
- **Question images:** in the editor, click a question's image box, then paste (Ctrl+V), drop a file, or click to pick one. Images show on the host screen.
- **Types:** Quiz (2 to 4 answers, one or more correct) and True/False. Each question has its own timer and Standard, Double or No points.
- **Backup:** **⬇** downloads a quiz as a `.json` file (theme and images included). **📂 Import file** loads it back, or loads a CSV with columns `question,a,b,c,d,correct,time,points`.

## Running the game on Zoom

1. On `host.html`, click **▶ Host**.
2. In Zoom, **Share Screen → the browser window**, and tick **Share sound**.
3. Click **Copy invite for Zoom chat** and paste it into the chat. The link has the PIN filled in.
4. Click **Start** when everyone is in. A 3-2-1 intro plays, then the questions begin. Press **Space**, **Enter** or **→** for Next.

What players see:
- **Laptop:** the question and the answer texts on coloured tiles.
- **Phone:** only the four coloured shapes, like Kahoot. They read the question from your Zoom share.

## Avatars

Players pick from 48 cartoon characters in 6 sets: Heroes, Cartoon, Smiles, Toons, Robots and People, plus a background colour.

**To add your own pictures:**
1. Copy them into `avatars\custom\`.
2. Double-click **`Update avatars.bat`**. It shrinks big pictures to 256 px squares and adds a **Custom** tab at the front of the picker.
3. Upload the folder to Netlify again.

Any new folder inside `avatars\` becomes its own tab. Remember the Netlify link is public: anyone with the link can see the pictures.

## Scoring (same as Kahoot)

- Correct answer: `1000 × (1 − (answer time ÷ timer) ÷ 2)`. Answers inside 0.5 seconds get the full 1000, and a last-second answer gets 500. Double points scores ×2. Wrong answers and no answer score 0.
- Streak bonus (lobby option): +100 for each correct answer in a row after the first, up to +500. It's never doubled.

## If something goes wrong

- **A player gets "Connection to the host failed":** they still have the old version. Ask them to press Ctrl + F5 and join again.
- **The host page stays on "Opening room…" or "Reconnecting…":** the free PeerJS server is busy or limiting your connection. Wait a few minutes and reload. It limits a connection that opens many rooms in a short time.
- **A player refreshes or drops:** they rejoin and keep their score.
- **Your host tab closes:** open `host.html` again and click **Resume**. The same PIN comes back and players reconnect on their own.
- After the podium, **⬇ Results (CSV)** saves everyone's points per question.

## Files

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `index.html`, `js/player.js` | Player page |
| `host.html`, `js/host.js` | Host page: quizzes, editor, game screens |
| `js/common.js` | Avatars, themes, scoring, sounds, animations |
| `js/store.js` | Saving and importing quizzes, the Claude prompt |
| `js/avatars.js` | Avatar list, generated by `Update avatars.bat` |
| `js/config.js` | Connection settings |
| `js/link.js` | Sends game messages through the PeerJS server instead of direct links |
| `assets/bg/` | Background photos |
| `avatars/` | Avatar pictures, one folder per tab |
| `CREDITS.md` | Where the photos, characters and font come from |
| `_dev/`, `tools/` | Tests and helper scripts (not needed online, harmless if uploaded) |
